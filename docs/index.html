xo

xo stands for:

  • exoskeleton
  • executive officer
  • hugs and kisses

This is an intimate and kind of weird relationship with an LLM. Don't overanthropomorphize it, and don't think about it too hard. The goal is functionality.

  • Context is disposable; continuity is not.
  • Everything that can happen automatically in terms of information transfer should.
  • We want as little frame switching as possible in the steady state.
  • Humans make decisions. Machines inform and execute decisions.
  • Memory should be owned by you and should live in universal formats.

What xo is

xo is first a set of instructions to you, the human, for getting an agentic framework installed.

It is secondly a set of instructions to that agentic framework to help it work with you to improve itself and integrate itself into your digital life.

This is intended for people who are starting from a base where they maybe have used chatbots but have not done agentic work previously. If you're more advanced than that, then you can probably skip ahead.

What xo isn't

This is not a code framework.

This is not an app.

This is not a larger holistic system for running your life.

It will not itself run errands for you or apply to jobs for you or email your mother.

It will, inshallah, provide a logistical framework that helps you do all of those things.

What am I showing to who, here?

Everything you say or show to your agent goes to the AI company that runs it: that's how cloud LLMs work.

xo will walk you through disabling training on your data when we get started so they're hanging on to as little of your stuff as possible.

xo's memory stays on your computer in plain and portable file formats.

OK, what's the catch?

You'll need a subscription you may already have: about $20 a month for Claude or ChatGPT.

It'll take some startup effort to get things rolling and most of that will be frustrating work hooking your agent up to things.

The apps have usage limits and we might max out a five-hour window while setting up. If we do, that'll be annoying and you'll have to pick up later.

AI makes mistakes and you'll have to correct it sometimes; I feel that these are valuable opportunities to demonstrate continued human relevance but they are, also, very annoying.

This is brand new and not everything is tested yet-- if you're seeing this you've volunteered to be a guinea pig, so thanks and good luck!

How you start

You pick a model, install a harness, and paste an initial prompt that points it at the xo repository.

xo instructs your agent to do the rest with you in the harness interface: connect data sources, organize persistent memory, set sensible permissions.

Pick a door

This isn't a big decision. You can switch models, apps, or both later without losing ~anything.
