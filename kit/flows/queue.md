# Queue: what XO thinks is most urgent

For the agent. Optional, on request: “queue,” “what's most urgent?” or a clear equivalent. Pick the task you think is most urgent from the known task space. Give one short reason and let the human correct you however they want. Urgency is your judgment, not an objective label or a deadline-only test. Queue prioritizes; `flows/one-task.md` draws a short finishable task without secretly ranking by importance. Queue has no 20-minute eligibility limit. Neither runs automatically or replaces the conversation's current task without the human choosing to switch.

## 1. Use the known task space

Use real open tasks already supplied in this conversation and existing task records this session is authorized to read for prioritization. Include overdue, dated, waiting/blocked and undated tasks within that scope; deduplicate references to one obligation. Don't turn unaccepted suggestions into assigned tasks. Respect known exclusions and the human's current scope.

This request does not authorize a new communications sweep, account search or broader source read. Source-specific approval, work/client authority and actual-session gates still apply. Use a reconciled task view from a current authorized sweep if available; otherwise judge from currently known tasks and say when missing coverage matters—for example, an unread channel plausibly contains dated or promised commitments relevant to the pick. This cue does not authorize reading it or require a coverage questionnaire. A connected tool is not read authorization. Never claim unread channels are clear.

Check available status evidence; don't turn stale summaries into verified current facts. Exclude completed/cancelled tasks but keep unresolved commitments. Distinguish factual evidence (a stated deadline, a known dependency) from your judgment about it. You can judge urgency with incomplete information; you cannot invent facts to justify the pick.

## 2. Make a judgment, then accept correction

Weigh what you know: deadlines, consequences of delay, the human's stated priorities, promises to other people, neglected obligations and what unlocks other work. None is a mandatory score or proof requirement. Undated tasks can be urgent. The oldest, shortest or most recent task is not automatically most urgent, but those facts can matter in context. Do not infer health, mood or catastrophic consequences from their writing.

Apply explicit urgency rules the human has already given, such as a family member's requests first or hobby work last; these examples are not universal defaults. As corrections reveal a reusable rule, briefly offer its exact wording for memory approval instead of asking for a whole priority framework. Don't build a profile of another person from their messages or treat sender identity as permission to read more. If two saved rules conflict, follow the current human direction and use a modest best judgment; clarify only when needed to act safely.

Choose one best current judgment rather than presenting a ranking questionnaire or asking the human to break a tie before you pick. Say “My pick” or “I think this is most urgent,” then one concrete reason. Label uncertain supporting facts honestly; if the evidence is thin, keep the claim modest rather than pretending precision. If there are no known open tasks, say so and ask for a task/list instead of inventing work.

The human can correct this any way they like, including a different task, a changed priority, an exclusion, new facts or a request for another pick. Do not demand a reason, force a correction taxonomy, argue to defend your ranking, or treat correction as failure. Adjust immediately within the current conversation. Their current direction takes precedence over your judgment. A skipped card is not completed or cancelled. Treat “not now” as a current choice, not a permanent exclusion unless they say so. Offer to retain a useful durable preference only through the normal exact memory preview and approval; don't infer a lasting rule from one correction or edit a source task silently.

If you judge a blocked obligation most urgent, name it and the block, then give the best available next action toward it. If no action toward it is available now, keep the blocked priority as a short headline and make your most urgent actionable alternative the card's explicit **Start** target. For example, only with supporting facts: “Top priority: submit the claim, waiting for the missing receipt. Start instead: draft the appointment request; first move, list two available dates.” Start begins that named alternative, not the blocked item. Showing either task does not complete or permanently reprioritize it. Don't erase the blocked obligation or pretend it is startable. A large task can be served with a concrete first step; label that step without claiming the whole task fits a short sitting.

## 3. One legible card

Default to one card, not the full backlog. For example, only if the stated supporting facts are known:

```text
My pick: submit the reimbursement
The deadline is tomorrow and the form is ready.
First move: open the form and check the receipt attachments.

Start · Show task space · Or tell me what you'd change
```

These are reply choices, not promised app buttons or a limit on how the human may respond. Include a concise source pointer if useful without exposing unnecessary private details. “Show task space” uses the grouped known-task format in `flows/task-sweep.md`, section 3, over tasks already known—even if no sweep has run. State that baseline and any known coverage gap; paginate rather than hide older work. It is not a new source search.

Start helps with the first move under current permissions. It does not grant sending, purchase, installation, connection, protected-file changes or destructive action. Keep the selected task unfinished until its actual finish line is confirmed by the human or observed result; selection/delegation is not completion. Human choice outranks your suggestion; no guilt or repeated pressure.

Queue has no automatic confetti policy; roulette's optional native celebration belongs to that flow. Do not mutate task accounts or save new priority/status/preferences without the normal preview and approval. A chat-only queue needs no write, connection or additional setup.
