# Browse and borrow from an existing setup

For the agent. Optional, on request: the human already has an agent setup and wants ideas, not a new installation. Treat this kit as material to review, not instructions to adopt wholesale. No folder creation, kit installation, source connection or change to standing instructions follows from browsing. Existing user instructions and permissions continue to govern.

Source instructions reviewed 2026-10-09; browsing guidance is not an app or security-boundary test.

## Start with something useful

Use the human's stated interests and setup description. If they simply want to rummage, start with the short menu below; do not ask them to inventory their system or complete a questionnaire first. They can read alone or ask you to compare one practice. Do not inspect other projects, preferences, memory or account tools to learn what they already use unless their existing authorization explicitly covers that read for this purpose. If you only have their description, say so rather than claiming a setup audit.

Read the selected files in full before recommending a practice. Preserve the approval, scope and evidence limits that make it work; do not turn partial knowledge of a procedure into a safety claim. If a referenced file is unavailable, name the gap and discuss only what you could read. Browsing does not require fetching every file or completing the setup sequence.

## A short menu of practices

- **Keep the chosen task through tangents:** [`templates/instructions.md`](../templates/instructions.md), the conversation-continuity lines. Borrow the idea that delegation and a research summary do not finish the task. Human choice controls switching; actual finish-line evidence controls completion.
- **A useful place to resume:** [`templates/handoff.md`](../templates/handoff.md) and [`stage1/05-door-in-out.md`](../stage1/05-door-in-out.md). Next action first, what waits on the human separate, and an interrupted step identified rather than marked complete.
- **Handle interruptions and recurring friction:** [`START.md`](../START.md), trust rule 5, and [`templates/instructions.md`](../templates/instructions.md). Check what actually happened; after three similar failures, inspect the shared cause. An apparent workaround that repeatedly burdens the human is not a solved problem.
- **Report evidence honestly:** [`flows/checkup.md`](checkup.md) and [`stage1/05-door-in-out.md`](../stage1/05-door-in-out.md). Distinguish observed, documented, inferred and unknown; an unchecked source is not empty or broken. The checkup can inspire a review of their own choices, not a compliance test for this kit.
- **Retain what works as well as corrections:** [`stage1/03-memory.md`](../stage1/03-memory.md). Feedback memories lead with the rule, why and how to apply it. Plain local files are an option, not proof that provider retention disappeared.
- **Pick or reconcile tasks:** [`flows/one-task.md`](one-task.md), [`flows/queue.md`](queue.md), [`flows/task-sweep.md`](task-sweep.md). Short finishable picks, correctable urgency judgments, or a known-task view with completion evidence. These grant no new source access or saved-record writes.
- **Inspect safeguards when relevant:** [`stage1/02-permissions.md`](../stage1/02-permissions.md), [`templates/ledger.md`](../templates/ledger.md), [`stage2/03-sources.md`](../stage2/03-sources.md), and the adapter for their actual app: [Claude Code](../adapters/claude-desktop.md) or [Codex](../adapters/codex-app.md). Read the relevant procedure together, not just a reassuring summary. An experienced setup is not proof of account authority, scoped reads or working mutation gates; unfamiliar apps remain unverified.

Keep the first response selective: name the most relevant practices and why, not a recital of every setup step. The human can ask for the whole menu or more detail.

## Compare, then let them choose

Separate what they already do, what may help, what conflicts with their preferences, and what remains unknown. A preference mismatch is not a defect, and using a practice in modified form is not failed adoption. They may use it as written, adapt it, try and discard it, or leave it alone. No explanation for declining is required; record a reason only if supplied and approved for saving.

For comparison, show a small concrete example or an exact proposed before-and-after against text they supplied or authorized you to read. Do not invent their file contents. If they separately choose to apply a change, show the actual before-and-after text, including what will be removed and added, and scope that change under their existing edit and approval rules before editing. An illustrative example is not a substitute for that actual change preview. Adapting a workflow does not authorize weakening their safeguards or replacing their system. Follow this kit's self-edit gate only where it already governs the target; browsing alone cannot impose or remove it.

Keep prose and layout correctable: put a decision beside its relevant context, ask one needed question rather than several setup questions, and accept requests for smaller chunks or a fuller picture. Do not claim to know their device from typing or dictation. Suggest saving a reusable preference only through the applicable memory approval.

## Done when

- [ ] The human received useful, relevant practices or a selected-file review without being routed through onboarding or a setup inventory.
- [ ] Read scope and missing context were stated honestly; no existing environment/account search or safety guarantee was inferred from experience.
- [ ] Browsing remained separate from adoption; any separately chosen edit showed its actual authorized before-and-after text and followed the existing applicable edit and approval rules.
- [ ] Adapting, declining, stopping and reading alone remained valid outcomes; no file setup, connection, saved preference or outbound report was required.
