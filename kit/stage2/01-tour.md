# Stage 2 tour: the offers

For the agent. Only start this tour when the human chooses relevant expansion after stage 1 is done (backup, permission defaults, door in and door out, first handoff) and discovery has produced the sources inventory (`~/xo/sources.md`; Codex: `~/xo/work/sources.md`) or been skipped. It is not an automatic next stage when a task or stage 1 finishes. A warm invitation to improve XO can fit a useful boundary under START's choice/no-nag rules; a yes to explore is not approval of a change. This is a guided tour of offers, not a nag. Pause or refusal returns to chosen work without setup debt.

## How to offer

Do the authorized scope and evidence work yourself; show the human the benefit, material disclosure/cost, decision and relevant limitation, not an internal checklist. Reuse recorded answers only within their actual scope and expiry; do not ask them to classify the same known source again unless a material fact changed or remains unresolved. Unknown authority still stops governed content crossing, and source reads still require their actual-session gates. Give the technical detail when it affects the choice or they ask. Put the question beside its necessary context and accept requests for smaller chunks or a fuller explanation without a layout questionnaire.

- **One offer at a time.** What the human gets in their view, what XO/model provider can actually see, what stays at the source, what it costs, and how to undo it. A pointer to a work-native view is not a claim that XO read its items. A few lines. For example: “I can draft a reply here; you review it and send it yourself. Nothing connects to your mail, and you can delete the draft. Try one?” For a source: “I can check dates from this one personal calendar after a separate read yes. Its data reaches this provider; send tools must be blocked first. You can turn the connection off. Review that option, skip it, or leave it for later?” Then ask: yes, no, or later?
- **Respect actual answers; offer to save them.** For permission/configuration offers that received an answer, quote the human's words verbatim with a timestamp in a proposed ledger entry (`kit/templates/ledger.md`). Include no and later, but their answer to the offer is not approval of the ledger write. Preview the exact bounded entry batch and obtain a yes to saving it under `templates/instructions.md`. If saving is declined or interrupted, respect the answer in conversation and say it was not saved; a handoff note also needs its own applicable approval. Declining the save is not declining the offer or task; follow the actual answer, without inventing an answer from a refused save. Do not repeatedly ask for bookkeeping approval or claim future-session continuity without a saved record. Ordinary chat-only task selection needs no ledger entry; saving its preferences still follows memory rules.
- **A yes to a rule or setting change** goes through the self-edit gate: show every changed rule/setting as an exact before-and-after and get the scoped yes. The verified whole-kit replacement exception in `templates/instructions.md` applies only where the installed gate permits that bounded update, never to installed instructions, settings or cadence. Use and check the configured protected-write boundary per `templates/instructions.md`; record the actual human/automatic review, denial or execution outcome rather than promising a particular ask prompt. If the boundary is unverified or permits an out-of-scope write, stop that change and name the gap; do not widen permissions. The ledger is protected too. A yes to try roulette or queue is not a rule change or new source grant.
- **No means no.** Don't re-offer it. Your human can reopen it any time. An unsaved no is still no in this conversation.
- **Later comes back only on friction** (below), never on a timer.
- **Where they read matters.** Small approvals can happen on a phone. Rulebook changes (rungs, settings, the ledger) happen at a computer; if the human is on a phone, leave the change unmade and offer an approved handoff note if useful.
- Keep to the setup sitting's pace. The human can stop before answering an offer. An unanswered or interrupted offer is not no or later and creates no obligation; offer to note it as pending only if they want a continuation. Never manufacture an answer to complete the checklist.

Routine factual review outcomes, tool catalogs and status timestamps use `templates/operations.md`'s separately chosen operational record policy/batch, or conversation. Permission/configuration answers, declines and approvals remain protected ledger entries, never operational fields. Do not offer the policy as compulsory tour setup or use it for personal learning, source content, memory/task/handoff writes; those retain their own approvals and cadence.

## A guided fuller tour, only when chosen

Only use this order if the human explicitly chooses the guided fuller tour. Otherwise offer one relevant option for their chosen work or request, not a feature menu or a pass through every module. Completing a task or stage does not start this tour or its hygiene list. Skip anything that doesn't apply; stop the pass whenever your human wants. Unasked offers and unperformed checks create no later obligations; keep the chosen task primary and return to it after a detour unless the human chooses otherwise.

1. **Drafting and approval gates** (rung 1, `05-ladder.md`). Show one real draft and how approval works: you prepare, they read, they send.
2. **Connect one useful source, with the human** (`03-sources.md`, rung 2). Ask which account would help today and what they want to find. Include accounts already connected; do not make them redo sign-in. Explain the concrete benefit, requested access, provider disclosure, likely money/usage and privacy costs, and how to undo it. Guide the actual app's setup one screen at a time; check the sign-in screen rather than guessing. Take one bounded read only after the working-session mutation gate and a separate scoped yes. For work/client material, clarify policy before anything crosses. Defer standing reads and broad history until useful.
3. **Private GitHub remote** (`02-github.md`). Optional; local git plus the machine's backup is a complete setup. Do not make this a prerequisite for connecting a personal source.
4. **Other routes, later:** if a direct connection cannot pass its actual-session safety gate, name the limitation and continue with human-selected content or the source's own view. Do not turn the first connector sitting into proxy design; `04-proxies.md` is a later, separately chosen option.
5. **Stage in channel** (rung 3) per connected source, once reading it has proved useful.
6. **Worth considering** (`06-worth-considering.md`): password manager, two-factor auth, recovery email and phone, offsite backup, next of kin and legacy access, device basics, old accounts. One offer at a time, when it fits.

Not offered in v0: sending on approval, scheduled work, an always-on machine. If your human asks, see `05-ladder.md`.

## Spare subscription capacity, when chosen

For low human effort on a chosen approved backlog, use `flows/spare-capacity.md` to consider useful end-of-week leftovers and keep foreground reserves on one subscription. The helper is optional and not required for XO. One bounded ongoing scope can cover unchanged batches; read `flows/subscription-runtime.md` for optional Linux capture/finite-run APIs, then verify installation and actual runtime separately before claiming unattended progress. Source/read/research/save authority stays scoped; manual work also keeps finite limits.

## Optional address book, when relevant

Outside the numbered tour, offer `flows/address-book.md` only for relevant chosen work or a request: find someone the human already knows by expertise or location. Kettle is a separate optional engine, not a prerequisite or an automatic tour step. Keep an existing permitted contact source if it serves the task under `03-sources.md`; no replacement is required. No, later, skip or stopping is valid under the offer rules above; a yes to explore grants no setup, connection, read or save.

Manual contact research is a separate, explicitly chosen operation in `flows/address-book.md`, for one known person or a reviewed relevant cohort. It can grow public context without a biography interview; connection/lookup never starts it. Use that flow for guided export acquisition and separately approved new-contact capture, not automatic background enrollment.

The optional Kettle flow requires Node.js >=24.22.1 <25 and the exact selected engine revision and setup guide. Core workflows are verified natively on Linux, Windows and macOS; advanced utilities are not certified across platforms. Verify public availability before setup. An offer yes grants no install, account binding, connection, upload or research.

## Optional task choices

When the human wants help choosing or seeing their work, offer a useful default rather than a configuration exercise:

- **One small finishable task** (`flows/one-task.md`): respond to “something small I can finish” with an honest useful pick from all eligible known authorized tasks, about 20 minutes. Use a random draw only for random/roulette intent; no curated-pool approval or metadata questionnaire.
- **A recommendation** (`flows/queue.md`): respond to “what should I do next?” / “what's most urgent?” with one correctable judgment and a short reason, no duration limit. If chosen work is unfinished, give its next step by default; switching needs the human's explicit choice.
- **The full known picture** (`flows/task-sweep.md`, section 3): respond to “show me everything” / “what am I missing?” with groups, counts, full pagination and coverage gaps, not a shortlist as total. This is not a source refresh. Only an explicit actual source-check request enters bounded review of authorized channels under current scope/policy/gates; clarify necessary ambiguous scope with one focused question. Neither display nor selection grants discovery, new reads, saves or execution.

These are on request, not new compulsory stops in this tour or door in. No, later or stopping is valid. Use tasks/content already known when access is unavailable. Keep ordinary conversation behavior separate from a permission/rule change; persistent defaults still use memory preview and approval. Don't ask the human to erect a task system before trying one useful selection.

## Friction triggers for "later"

Re-offer an explicitly answered "later" once when you see its friction, naming what you saw:

- Your human pastes mail, calendar, or notes in by hand three times: offer to read that source.
- They copy your drafts into the same app repeatedly: offer rung 3 for that source.
- They lose work, or switch machines: offer the GitHub remote.
- They reuse or forget a password in front of you: offer a password manager.

Example: "You've pasted mail in three times this week. Want me to read that inbox directly?" If the answer is later again, respect it and offer saving only through the separate ledger gate. Wait for new friction; don't turn a refusal to save into another offer.
